How to Play & Runic Rules

Master the runic keyboard before words reach the abyss

Keyboard Typing: Type letters, syllables and words before they hit the bottom of the screen
Wildcard Words: Instantly destroy all runics on screen simultaneously and absorb all their points
Keys 1–8 (or click): Activate slow motion, freeze, spacing, shield and power spells
Combos & Fury Mode: Error-free streaks multiply your score up to 8×
Shields & Danger: Runics at the bottom flash red and damage 1 shield if dropped (3 shields in Classic)
